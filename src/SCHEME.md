This opportunity is a direct match for what I do: driving execution of a Customer Data, Analytics and AI delivery roadmap end-to-end, coordinating business, technical and vendor teams under Agile delivery — exactly the scope described for this role. The engagement's shape also matters: a 6-month, extendable cover assignment for an already-active EMEA Data, Analytics & AI portfolio is a familiar type of work for me — stepping into a running delivery motion and keeping it moving through a transition period, without a ramp-up curve and without loss of governance discipline.

## Project · Proposal {nav="Project"}

### How I'd keep this portfolio moving without a ramp-up curve.

#### The problem

The underlying need is most likely continuity risk. A defined-duration "Maternity Cover" for an active, multi-vendor Customer Data, Analytics & AI portfolio suggests the organization needs committed roadmap initiatives, vendor coordination and stakeholder governance to keep moving without interruption during the transition — not a new strategy defined from scratch.

#### The proposed solution

Apply a proven multi-vendor delivery governance model — Agile backlog, planning and release coordination; structured risk, dependency and escalation management; and continuous alignment between business, technical and vendor teams — a model that travels across data platform types rather than being tied to one specific tool.

#### Conceptual architecture

*engagement governance for the covered period — not the underlying platform's technical architecture*

- **Cadence:** weekly delivery and risk review; recurring vendor/delivery-partner coordination checkpoint; stakeholder steering touchpoint for scope and trade-off decisions.
- **Handover:** a structured handover at the end of the covered assignment, planned from day one — not an end-of-contract scramble.
- **Vendor/delivery-partner governance:** the same multi-vendor coordination model applied throughout the cover period — holding delivery partners accountable to scope, schedule and quality, not just tracking status.
- **Boundary, stated explicitly:** this architecture governs delivery execution and vendor coordination on any data platform. It does not extend to hands-on configuration of a specific named Customer Data Platform (CDP) or applied AI/ML tooling — both nice-to-have in the role's own scope, not a must-have, and outside what this engagement is built to govern.
